💡 Excellence & innovation
Originality, conceptual rigor, and creative integration across all three layers.

Weight: 20%

Judges assess:

Innovative multi-layer architecture linking agents, DKG, and trust mechanisms
Originality of agent behavior: e.g., AI agents that detect misinformation, verify truth, or transact autonomously
Use of Polkadot interoperability and shared security to empower new AI functions (cross-chain reasoning, governance, oracles)
Depth of analysis and accuracy: sound judgment, factual precision, and clear reasoning behind claims or verifications
Conceptual clarity and visual logic (architecture diagrams, reasoning workflows)
Potential to advance the NeuroWeb–Polkadot trust layer
Scoring guide: 5 – Groundbreaking & technically advanced | 4 – Highly innovative & clear | 3 – Good but incremental | 2 – Limited originality | 1 – Weak concept

⚙️ Technical implementation & code quality
Execution depth, interoperability, and clarity.

Weight: 40%

Judges assess:

Functional implementation of your project
Use of the DKG Edge Node (hard requirement)
Use of DKG Knowledge Assets (discoverable, linked Knowledge Assets, valid JSON-LD/RDF)
Code quality, documentation, and architecture clarity
Scoring guide: 5 – Fully functional & interoperable prototype | 4 – Working with minor issues | 3 – Prototype or partial integration | 2 – Limited functionality | 1 – Minimal execution

💥 Impact & relevance
Societal, ecosystem, and multi-chain impact.

Weight: 20%

Judges assess:

Contribution to the theme (Scaling Trust in the Age of AI) through cross-chain knowledge integrity
Real-world value: misinformation defense, decentralized governance, identity verification, or transparent data economy
Measurable outcomes — e.g., improved accuracy, verifiable provenance, or enhanced ecosystem usability
Scoring guide: 5 – Transformative multi-chain impact | 4 – Strong ecosystem contribution | 3 – Moderate but promising | 2 – Limited relevance | 1 – Minimal impact

⚖️ Ethics, sustainability & openness
Responsible, transparent, and open collaboration.

Weight: 10%

Judges assess:

Transparency and provenance (verifiable data, clear authorship, and auditability)
Use of open-source standards and interoperability principles
Alignment with human-centric values: openness, inclusion, and trustworthiness
Scoring guide: 5 – Exemplary ethical & sustainable design | 4 – Strong practices | 3 – Adequate awareness | 2 – Limited evidence | 1 – Absent consideration

🎬 Communication & presentation
Clarity and persuasiveness of storytelling.

Weight: 10%

Judges assess:

Clarity in demonstrating three-layer interoperability
Thoroughness of explanation — does the team clearly show how and why their system works?
Highlight of key insights or discrepancies (where relevant)
Visual clarity: effective use of diagrams, demos, and examples
Narrative engagement: connection between technical execution and societal purpose
Scoring guide: 5 – Outstanding clarity & storytelling | 4 – Strong & engaging | 3 – Clear but unpolished | 2 – Hard to follow | 1 – Poor or incomplete
